There are three types of data types in SQL. 
1. Numerical
    a. INT.
    b. DECIMAL. 

2. TEXT:
    a. CHAR: stored FIXED size. 
    b. VARCHAR: We mentioned the upper limit here. 
    c. TEXT: can stored up to 65,535 characters
    d. MEDIUMTEXT: can stored up to 16,777,215 characters.
    e. LONGTEXT: 4,294,967,295 characters

3. Temporal:
    a. DATE. 
    b. TIME.
